Profile

Hi, I’m Scott.

I’ve spent the last 20+ years building software across startups and large organizations, mostly in cybersecurity, including Immunet, Sourcefire, Cisco, Symantec, and TELUS.

I started by automating repetitive operational work and grew into building endpoint security software, distributed systems, and cloud-based services. Over time, I found that one of my strongest contributions is simplifying complex systems, aligning teams, and making technical ideas easier to work with.

My work has spanned the full development lifecycle, from low-level client software to scalable backend services, and I’ve often stepped into informal leadership roles to guide projects, mentor teammates, and bring clarity to ambiguous problems.

Today, I’m focused on roles that combine technical depth with product thinking, communication, and real-world impact — especially in environments using AI to solve meaningful problems.

I still build, but the way I approach it has changed. I use AI-assisted development tools to move faster across unfamiliar technologies, while relying on my experience to guide architecture, validate output, and ensure what gets built actually works.

Current Direction

I’m especially interested in roles at the intersection of technology, people, and product, including:

  • Technical Program Manager / Technical Lead
  • Product Manager — technical or AI-focused
  • Solutions Engineer / Solutions Architect
  • Technical Customer Success
  • Applied AI / AI Product Engineering
  • Developer Experience / Internal Tools

I’m particularly drawn to companies using AI to solve meaningful problems in areas like learning, health, decision-making, governance, or systems that help people understand complex information.

Modern Development Approach

I see coding as changing. The value is no longer only in manually writing every line, but in knowing what to build, how systems should fit together, how to validate the result, and how to use powerful AI tools responsibly to move faster.

  • Use AI-assisted development tools, including Codex-style workflows and LLMs, to rapidly prototype and build across unfamiliar stacks
  • Combine 20+ years of engineering experience with AI tools to dramatically increase speed, flexibility, and scope
  • Focus on system design, problem framing, architecture, validation, and usefulness rather than manual implementation of every component
  • Bring enough technical depth to guide AI-generated work, spot problems, understand tradeoffs, and turn prototypes into maintainable solutions
