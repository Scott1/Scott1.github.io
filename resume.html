Profile

Software engineer with 20+ years of experience building security-focused systems across startups and enterprise environments, including Immunet, Sourcefire, Cisco, Symantec, and TELUS.

I started by automating repetitive operational work and grew into developing endpoint security software, distributed systems, and cloud-based services. Over time, I discovered that one of my strongest contributions is simplifying complex systems, aligning teams, and communicating technical ideas clearly.

I’ve worked across the full development lifecycle, from low-level client software to scalable backend services, while frequently stepping into informal leadership roles to guide projects, mentor teammates, and bring clarity to ambiguous problems.

I’m especially interested in roles where technology is used to solve meaningful problems, particularly in AI, health, learning, and systems that help people understand and interact with complex information.

Key Takeaways

Across these roles, a few patterns stand out. Clear ownership and alignment matter as much as process. Structure improves consistency but does not guarantee clarity. Culture and proximity to meaningful work have enormous impact on engagement and performance.

Some of my most valuable work has come not just from writing code, but from seeing the bigger picture, translating complexity, mentoring others, and helping teams move forward when the path wasn’t obvious.
