Professional Summary

I’ve spent more than two decades building software across telecom, cybersecurity, startup, acquisition, and enterprise environments, including TELUS, Symantec, Immunet, Sourcefire, and Cisco.

Technically, I’ve worked across client systems, backend services, installers, build systems, cloud infrastructure, APIs, observability, and distributed systems. Humanly, I’ve often been at my best where things are ambiguous: helping people align, making complex systems understandable, mentoring, reducing confusion, and moving teams through stuck places.

I’m most interested in work that combines technical depth with meaning, especially where AI, learning, health, governance, systems design, and human flourishing intersect.
