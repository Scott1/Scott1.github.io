Projects

These are less about portfolio polish and more about what genuinely captures my imagination.

Governance

Blockchain-Based Voting System

I’m exploring how blockchain architecture, citizen authentication, oracle layers, and zero-knowledge proofs might support secure, transparent, privacy-preserving elections.

What excites me is not just digitizing the current system, but rethinking what democratic participation could become. With the technology now available, I think we could dramatically increase voter turnout, make voting far more frequent, and eventually move beyond only electing representatives toward more direct voting on issues themselves.

To me that points toward a truer representative democracy: not just choosing someone to speak for you every few years, but creating a system where everyone can more directly have their voice heard on the questions that shape collective life.

This project matters to me because governance is not abstract. It affects whether societies can function with legitimacy, fairness, trust, and real participation.

Status: active concept / architecture exploration

Education

AI for Learning and Civic Understanding

I’m exploring how AI can help all of us learn more effectively, not just in classrooms, but throughout life. Yes, it could dramatically improve student learning and reduce the load on educators, and I think we should be moving aggressively to get these tools into people’s hands where they are genuinely helpful.

I’m especially interested in how this connects to civic life. Easier and more frequent voting is only truly valuable if people also have better ways to understand government, the principles behind it, what is actually happening, why it matters, and how decisions are being made.

I think AI could help people become not just more informed, but more capable participants in public life.
