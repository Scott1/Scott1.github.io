Why I’m here

I don’t want a life built around approval, status, or pretending to be smaller than I am.

I want to bring my whole self to the table: the engineer, the artist, the systems thinker, the dreamer, the person who has spent a lot of time working inwardly and now wants that inner work to become something useful in the world.

I care about service. I care about helping. I care about building things that reduce confusion, reduce suffering, create dignity, and help more people flourish in their own way.

“I don’t want to fit into what is available. I want to meet the work that is truly mine to do.”

What feels true

I love people. I love working with people. I love solving problems with people.

I love talking, laughing, feeling, thinking, and building together. Some of my strongest work comes from making other people feel seen, helping them move through confusion, and turning tangled situations into something more coherent and alive.

What I’m moving toward

I want to help build a world where technology serves life, not the other way around.

I’m especially drawn to AI, learning, health, decision-making, governance, and any work that could help humanity become more compassionate, wise, peaceful, and capable of shared flourishing.
