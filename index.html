Technology • Creativity • Service • Human Possibility

I’m an artist at heart working through technology.

I’ve spent more than 20 years in software, but what matters most to me is not status, polish, or fitting into a role. I’m a creative, a dreamer, and someone who cares deeply about people, meaning, imagination, and building things that could genuinely help the world become more compassionate, peaceful, and alive.

What feels true

I love people. I love working with people. I love solving problems with people.

I’m at my best where technical depth, emotional honesty, systems thinking, and human connection all meet. I like making things clearer, lighter, and more possible for the people around me.

What I’m moving toward

I want technology to serve life, not the other way around.

I’m especially drawn to AI, learning, health, decision-making, governance, and any work that could help humanity become wiser, kinder, and more capable of shared flourishing.
